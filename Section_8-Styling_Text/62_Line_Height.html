Line Height — Realistic Example

This is an example of tight line-height. Notice how the lines are very close to each other, making the paragraph feel packed and a little hard to read. In real websites, this type of spacing is rarely used for body text because readers feel uncomfortable when the lines don’t have enough breathing room.

This is the most common and comfortable line-height used in paragraphs. The spacing between each line feels natural, making it easier for readers to follow long blocks of text without feeling tired. Most blogs, articles, and modern websites use a line-height between 1.4 and 1.6 because it improves readability and looks clean on all screen sizes.

This paragraph demonstrates loose line-height. The lines have a lot more space between them, giving the text an open and airy feeling. Designers often use this type of spacing for hero sections, large headings, or minimalistic layouts where extra spacing is preferred to make the text stand out and look elegant on the page.
